Fixes the preview workflow the previous reporter described: the node auto-fits its height to the content (that is why dragging the corner snapped back), and the preview tiles were fixed-size.

**What is new.** A tile-size switch in the header, next to undo: one click cycles M / L / XL / S. Fewer tiles per row at the larger steps, so reviewing a big pile of styles takes a fraction of the scrolling, and the choice is remembered per browser. All chip grids and the results dock follow the same switch.

The node keeps auto-fitting its height to the content (more picks, taller node), which is the reliable cross-frontend behavior; the tile-size switch is the way to a bigger preview.
